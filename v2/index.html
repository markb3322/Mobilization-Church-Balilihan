About Us

Mobilization Church Balilihan – A Home for Every Believer.

Mobilization Church Balilihan is a growing, spirit-filled Born Again Christian organization dedicated to spreading the Gospel and making disciples in Balilihan and beyond. Our system is designed to bridge technology and ministry, streamlining membership registration, data management, and activity coordination so we can focus on spiritual growth and community building.

Through our digital platform, leaders can effectively track church growth, maintain transparent communication between fellowships, and ensure every member is engaged in discipleship tracks and church updates.

Why are you waiting to join us?

Finding a spiritual home where you can authentically grow is one of the most important decisions you can make. Our church provides a safe haven for seekers and believers alike, extending a warm, non-judgmental embrace to everyone who wants to experience God's transforming love.

Don't hesitate to take your next step of faith. By being a part of our church, you are not just attending services; you are becoming part of a loving family that prays together, serves together, and walks the journey of life side by side.

Our 10 Key Ministries & Activities

1. Sunday Worship

We gather every Sunday to praise and worship the Lord together through vibrant singing and the study of His Word.

2. Discipleship & Life Groups

Smaller groups meet for deeper Bible study, mutual support, and spiritual growth in faith.

3. Community Outreach

We share God's love outside the church through feeding programs, gift-giving, and helping those in need.

4. Men's Fellowship

A fellowship for men to strengthen each other's faith, become good family leaders, and grow in God's word.

5. Women's Fellowship

A gathering of women for prayer, Bible study, and inspiring one another as partners in ministry.

6. Youth Fellowship

A vibrant community for young people to know Christ, develop talents, and build meaningful friendships.

7. Praise & Worship Team

A group of singers and musicians dedicated to leading the congregation in deep and sincere worship.

8. Night Revival

Nights filled with fervent prayer, deep worship, and renewing the fire of faith.

9. Youth Camp

An annual outdoor church activity where young people experience a deeper encounter with God.

10. Sunday School

Providing foundational Christian education to children through engaging Bible stories and creative arts.

Vision, Mission & Goals

Our Vision

To be a Christ-centered community impacting Balilihan, Bohol, and the nations with the transforming love of God.

Our Mission

To make disciples, plant churches, and demonstrate God's kingdom through spiritual and social relevance in our municipality.

Our Goals

Train 100 church leaders annually, establish community care centers, and foster unity among Christian sectors in Balilihan.

Policies & Regulations

Data Privacy & Protection

We value your privacy. All registration details collected through the Mobilization Church system are stored in a secured environment solely utilized for church fellowship communications and membership records. We do not distribute or share your personal data with third-party organizations.

Community Guidelines

Members are expected to engage in mutual respect in line with Christian doctrines. Any form of harassment, malicious communication, or activities violating church by-laws reflected within church physical premises or digital threads strictly warrant appropriate pastoral counseling or organizational review.
